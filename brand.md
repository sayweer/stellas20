# Brand — Everspan

Everspan separates yield-bearing assets into tradable principal and yield positions on Stellar.

_Generated by `brand-design` on 2026-08-01._

## Palette — Signal Blue

**Vibe:** minimal · serious · premium · trustworthy  
**Category:** DeFi  
**Mood:** minimal + serious

### Seeds

| Role         | Hex       | Job                                                      |
| ------------ | --------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| bg-base      | `#F5F3F4` | Paper. The marketing surface and the light app canvas.    |
| bg-elevated  | `#EDEBF0` | One step down from paper for cards and inset rows.        |
| primary      | `#3E56FD` | Everspan blue — primary actions and brand moments.        |
| primary-soft | `#BAC3FE` | Pale blue for small marks on dark surfaces.               |
| deep         | `#0C0146` | Navy. Panels and elevated layers on the black canvas.     |
| fg-base      | `#050505` | Black. The marketing band, dark chapters, app canvas.     |

Black is deliberately kept beside the three seeds rather than replaced by the
navy: the opening band and the dark chapters are black, and navy panels read as
a layer standing above that black rather than as the surface itself.

### Surface roles

Three tokens sit beside the neutral ramp, each owning one job so no ramp step
has to serve two surfaces at once:

| Token      | Job                                                       |
| ---------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| `boundary` | Edge of an interactive control. Carries 3:1 non-text contrast. |
| `hairline` | Decorative edge — card outlines and dividers. Matches ink/10. |
| `raised`   | Fill of a control that is selected, hovered, or a skeleton. |

### Radii

Three steps, no others. `rounded-full` for anything you press — text buttons,
segmented controls, chips, icon buttons. `rounded-xl` for anything holding
content — inputs, rows, nested boxes, skeletons. `rounded-2xl` for a panel.

### Contrast check

| Pair                     | Ratio                                      |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------ |
| ink / paper              | 17.9:1 ✓                                   |
| muted ink / paper        | 6.1:1 ✓                                    |
| white / primary blue     | 5.3:1 ✓                                    |
| primary blue / ink       | 4.0:1 — large text and graphics only       |
| accent-300 / ink         | 8.1:1 ✓                                    |

Use white text on blue fills. Never use black text on the primary blue. On black surfaces, use the blue for large graphics and `accent-300` for small text and focus indicators.

## Typography — Space Grotesk

- **Display + body:** Space Grotesk Variable
- **Mono:** system monospace stack

Headlines are large, compact and restrained. Numbers use `font-mono tabular-nums`.

## Gradients (not used)

The minimal, serious direction relies on flat color fields, spacing and typography. Gradients would dilute the identity.

## Tone and voice

Use direct, factual language. Describe the position, rate and maturity before describing the technology. Prefer short sentences and concrete numbers.

Avoid hype, urgency, exclamation marks and claims such as “revolutionary”, “effortless” or “risk-free”. Never imply guaranteed protocol safety or guaranteed yield.

> One deposit. Two positions. Choose the rate exposure you want to hold.

## Usage dos and don'ts

**Do:**

- Use paper `#F5F3F4` as the default marketing surface, black for high-contrast chapters, navy `#0C0146` for panels above black, and `#3E56FD` for primary actions and brand moments.
- Keep one blue action per viewport whenever possible.
- Use a 4 px spacing grid and large, intentional whitespace.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` and keep all content usable without animation.

**Don't:**

- Hardcode brand colors in component files; use Tailwind tokens or CSS variables.
- Use the brand blue for positive/negative market semantics. Blue means Everspan, not direction; carry meaning with icons and copy.
- Add decorative gradients, glassmorphism or excessive shadows.
- Copy Paribu assets, text or exact layouts; translate the visual principles into Everspan’s product story.

The neutral ramp runs along the line from the paper seed to the navy seed, so every grey belongs to the palette instead of sitting beside it. `950` stays black on its own.
