### Surface roles

Three tokens sit beside the neutral ramp, each owning one job so no ramp step
has to serve two surfaces at once:

| Token      | Job                                                       |
| ---------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| `boundary` | Edge of an interactive control. Carries 3:1 non-text contrast. |
| `hairline` | Decorative edge — card outlines and dividers. Matches ink/10. |
| `raised`   | Fill of a control that is selected, hovered, or a skeleton. |

### Radii

Three steps, no others. `rounded-full` for anything you press — text buttons,
segmented controls, chips, icon buttons. `rounded-xl` for anything holding
content — inputs, rows, nested boxes, skeletons. `rounded-2xl` for a panel.

### Contrast check

| Pair                | Ratio                                      |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| ink / paper         | 20.38:1 ✓                                  |
| muted ink / paper   | 7.46:1 ✓                                   |
| paper / primary red | 8.52:1 ✓                                   |
| primary red / ink   | 2.39:1 — decorative or large graphics only |

Use white text on red fills. Never use black text on the primary red. On black surfaces, use red for large graphics; use the lighter red ramp for small focus indicators.

## Typography — Space Grotesk

- **Display + body:** Space Grotesk Variable
- **Mono:** system monospace stack

Headlines are large, compact and restrained. Numbers use `font-mono tabular-nums`.

## Gradients (not used)

The minimal, serious direction relies on flat color fields, spacing and typography. Gradients would dilute the identity.

## Tone and voice

Use direct, factual language. Describe the position, rate and maturity before describing the technology. Prefer short sentences and concrete numbers.

Avoid hype, urgency, exclamation marks and claims such as “revolutionary”, “effortless” or “risk-free”. Never imply guaranteed protocol safety or guaranteed yield.

> One deposit. Two positions. Choose the rate exposure you want to hold.

## Usage dos and don'ts

**Do:**

- Use pure white as the default marketing surface, black for high-contrast chapters and `#9B0F06` for primary actions and brand moments.
- Keep one red action per viewport whenever possible.
- Use a 4 px spacing grid and large, intentional whitespace.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` and keep all content usable without animation.

**Don't:**

- Hardcode brand colors in component files; use Tailwind tokens or CSS variables.
- Use red for positive/negative market semantics. Brand red means Everspan, not loss.
- Add decorative gradients, glassmorphism or excessive shadows.
- Copy Paribu assets, text or exact layouts; translate the visual principles into Everspan’s product story.

The neutral ramp is warm from `400` down; `50`–`300` are neutral so pure white never reads as yellowed against the step below it.
